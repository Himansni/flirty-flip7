Three Principles for Deeper Connection

How FlirtyFlip structures question gameplay so conversations feel safe, balanced, and uplifting.

1

The Mutual Skip Rule

Consent and comfort come first. Either partner can pass on any card with zero pressure and zero questions asked.

2

Alternating Turns

Personalized turns ensure neither partner dominates the conversation. Equal space is held for both voices.

3

Curiosity Over Correction

Prompts are open-ended catalysts. Listen to understand and appreciate your partner’s inner world rather than debate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about FlirtyFlip relationship question decks.

How many questions are available in FlirtyFlip decks?
FlirtyFlip features 10 themed question decks containing 499 total prompt cards, covering sweet romance, playful banter, deep emotional vulnerability, and future goals.
Can we choose how many questions to play in one session?
Yes. Each deck allows you to choose your round length: a quick 10-card connection round, a 25-card date night session, or a full 50-card immersive experience.
What happens if a question feels uncomfortable or too intense?
Mutual consent is fundamental to FlirtyFlip. Every card has an immediate skip button. Either partner can pass on any prompt at any time without question or consequence.
Are there different question decks for dating vs married couples?
Yes. Decks like Sweet and How Well Do You Know Me? are great for new couples and casual dates, while Romantic, Intimate, and Dreams & Future offer deeper reflection suited for long-term partners.
Do we have to pay or install an app to browse and play question decks?
No. You can play directly in your browser for free as a guest on any mobile phone, tablet, or desktop with no app installation required.
