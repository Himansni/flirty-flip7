Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about playing FlirtyFlip online and on mobile devices.

Is FlirtyFlip free to play?
Yes. You can play card decks and couple mini-games on any device directly in your browser for free as a guest, without downloading an app or paying a subscription.
Do we need an account to play?
No account is required for local pass-and-play on a single shared device. To play Online Games with private room codes across two separate devices, a free sign-in is required to synchronize your session.
How many games and decks are available on FlirtyFlip?
FlirtyFlip features 10 question decks containing 711 unique prompts, plus 9 interactive couple mini-games including Would You Rather, Tic-Tac-Toe, Couple Dice, and Rapid Fire.
Can we skip questions or activities we are not comfortable with?
Yes. Every question, challenge, and mini-game on FlirtyFlip is strictly optional. Either partner can pass or skip any card at any time without question.
How does mature content work on FlirtyFlip?
Mature decks such as Spicy 18+, Intimate, and Dark Desire require an adult confirmation gate (both players confirming 18+ and mutual consent) before starting. Gentle decks like Sweet, Romantic, and Playful launch immediately without any age prompt.
