Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing FlirtyFlip in a long-distance relationship.

How do online games work for long distance couples on FlirtyFlip?
One partner signs in and creates a room at /online, generating a private 6-character room code. The other partner joins using that code, allowing both players to see synchronized cards and turn actions in real time.
Do both of us need to download an application?
No. FlirtyFlip runs directly in modern mobile and desktop web browsers (Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox). Neither partner needs to install an app from an app store.
Do we need an account for long distance play?
Yes. While local pass-and-play on one device can be played freely as a guest, online rooms require a free sign-in to securely authenticate players, connect private room channels, and prevent strangers from entering your room.
Which games can be played remotely online?
FlirtyFlip offers 7 server-synchronized mini-games online: Tic-Tac-Toe, Love Toss, Couple Wheel, Rapid Fire, Mystery Box, Couple Dice, and Choose a Door, plus online question card rooms.
Can we play across different devices like iPhone and laptop?
Yes. FlirtyFlip is fully responsive and cross-platform. One partner can play on an iPhone while the other plays on an Android phone, Mac, or Windows PC.
