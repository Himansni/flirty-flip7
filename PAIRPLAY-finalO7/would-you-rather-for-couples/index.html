The Secret-Vote & Match Reveal Mechanic

Why FlirtyFlip’s dilemma engine is more engaging than reading a static list of questions.

1

Player 1 Votes in Secret

Player 1 sees the dilemma and selects Option A or Option B without letting their partner see the screen.

2

Device Passes Cleanly

The screen hides the selection and prompts Player 2 to take the phone for their secret turn.

3

Dramatic Match Reveal

Both choices are revealed side-by-side with partner name tags: “IT’S A MATCH!” or “Different Sides!”

4

Couple Discussion

Take 60 seconds to explain your choices. Did you guess what your partner would pick? Why did you choose your side?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about playing Would You Rather for couples.

How does the FlirtyFlip Would You Rather game work?
Player 1 makes their choice in secret without letting their partner see. Then the phone is passed to Player 2, who chooses. Once both have voted, the game reveals whether your choices matched or differed, followed by a couple discussion prompt.
How many Would You Rather questions are in the game?
FlirtyFlip features 24 curated dilemma prompt pairs covering romance, travel, quirks, lifestyle choices, and future dreams, with cryptographically secure random selection so you never get predictable rounds.
Can we play Would You Rather for free without downloading an app?
Yes. The Would You Rather game is 100% free to play directly in your web browser on any phone, tablet, or desktop with no account or app store download required.
Can we play Would You Rather with our own nicknames?
Yes. FlirtyFlip lets you personalize alternating turns with your names or nicknames, making turn transitions clear, fun, and personal.
Are the dilemmas safe for new couples?
Yes. The Would You Rather dilemmas are categorized and designed for all couples, focusing on fun scenarios, connection, and relationship preferences rather than explicit content.
